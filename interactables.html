Interactables

Interactables are objects that appear in the world during a run with different spawn chances. Stand near one to charge it up — the more players nearby, the faster it triggers — then it fires its effect.

How they work: Stay within range for a few seconds (faster with more players in range, and faster with the Interaction Time perk) to trigger the effect.

Heal

Spawns a Heal Bubble that restores HP.

Biomass

Spawns Biomass pickups. The amount scales with the current wave.

Speed Bubble

Spawns a bubble that temporarily boosts move speed.

Loot Magnet

Spawns a pickup that pulls nearby loot toward the player.

Enemy Spawn

Spawns Lord Rammhorn for the players to fight.

Boss Reward

Spawns bonus artifact loot, awarded after defeating a boss.
