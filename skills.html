Skills

There are 8 active skills. Each skill has purchasable Morphs (shop upgrades paid with Mycelium, plus Biomass/Amber for higher total investments) and can be upgraded via Evolutions across up to 4 tiers: Tier 0 (Targeting), Tier 1 (skill-specific), Tier 2 (special effect), and Tier 3 (generic stat boost).

Generic Evolutions

These options are available (with some restrictions) for every skill.

Tier 0 – Targeting & Bonus Damage

  • Target: Nearest Enemy
  • Target: Lowest HP
  • Target: Highest Max HP
  • Target: Furthest Enemy
  • Target: Flying Enemy
  • Bonus vs. Ground Enemies: ×1.5 damage
  • Bonus vs. Flying Enemies: ×1.5 damage
  • Bonus vs. Elite Enemies: ×1.5 damage

Tier 2 – Special Effects

  • Poison
    Poisons hit enemies for 5 seconds, ticking every 1 second for 20% of the initial hit damage.
  • Vampire
    Reduces your damage dealt by 20%. 1.5% of the reduced damage is returned as health.
  • Slow
    Slows hit enemies for 1.5 seconds. The strength varies per skill (see table below).
