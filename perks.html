In-Game Perks

The general perk pool, from which you can choose in addition to your character's level-up perks every time you level up. There are 15 stat types, each available in the rarities Common, Rare, and Epic (45 perk assets in total). A Legendary rarity exists but is not currently used in the perk pool.
