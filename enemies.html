Behavior Patterns

  • Melee walkers (Ant, Big Ant, Larva, Big Larva, Bork, Tick, Horn Beetle, Mantis): chase the nearest player and attack on contact.
  • Tick: slows down and unleashes a stronger attack once below 50% HP.
  • Ranged fliers (Flying Bug, Toxic Spitter): keep their distance, dart in to attack, then fly away to reposition.
  • Rollers (Cellar Beetle, Big Cellar Beetle): roll around the arena and damage players on collision.
  • Loot Bug: doesn't attack — wanders, flees when hit or approached, and drops bonus XP when damaged.
  • Greedle (event): doesn't attack — heads for the event objective, lingers, then leaves.
